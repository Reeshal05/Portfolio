I'm Reeshal — a curious, organised and creative person who likes turning messy ideas into clear plans.

People, ideas & making things happen.

My background is in Computer Science, but I found myself most excited by the parts of work that involve people, communication, ideas and execution.

Today, I'm building my path in brand & social marketing, while exploring founder support and creative operations.

I like creative work that still has a purpose — whether that's figuring out what a brand should say, planning content, organising a project or making a founder's life a little easier.
